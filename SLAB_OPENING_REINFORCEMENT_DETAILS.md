# SLAB OPENING REINFORCEMENT - COMPLETE TABLE (NTS)

## General Notes

- Small openings (d <= 150 mm): bars may be displaced; no additional reinforcement required.
- Openings > 150 mm: provide additional reinforcement at top and bottom layers.
- No U-bars are used; reinforcement consists of straight bars only.
- Interrupted bars are cut and replaced with straight bars of equivalent area.
- Provide the greater of:
  - Equivalent area of cut reinforcement, or
  - Values from the table below.
- All added and replacement bars must extend beyond the opening edge by the required tension development length (Ld).

---

## Opening Size Classification (d)

| Opening Size (d) | Classification | Requirement |
|------------------|----------------|-------------|
| d <= 150         | Small          | No additional reinforcement |
| 150 < d <= 450   | Medium         | Provide additional top and bottom reinforcement |
| d > 450          | Large          | Full detailing: Aa, Ab, and Adiag required |

---

## Reinforcement Requirements (Top and Bottom Layers)

| Opening Dimension (a) | Aa, perpendicular to a | Opening Dimension (b) | Ab, perpendicular to b | Adiag |
|----------------------|------------------------|----------------------|------------------------|-------|
| 150 < a <= 450       | 2T16                   | 150 < b <= 450       | 2T16                   | -     |
| 450 < a <= 1000      | 3T16                   | 450 < b <= 1000      | 3T16                   | 2T16  |
| 1000 < a <= 1500     | 4T18                   | 1000 < b <= 1500     | 4T18                   | 3T16  |
| a > 1500             | 5T18                   | b > 1500             | 5T18                   | 4T16  |

---

## Notes

- Aa: reinforcement perpendicular to dimension (a).
- Ab: reinforcement perpendicular to dimension (b).
- Adiag: diagonal corner reinforcement.
- All reinforcement is provided at both top and bottom layers.
- Provide the greater of:
  - Equivalent area of cut reinforcement, or
  - Values from the table.
- All bars must extend beyond the opening edge by development length (Ld).
- No U-bars; only straight reinforcement is used.
